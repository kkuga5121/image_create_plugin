# English Stock Metadata

Use the final accepted accessible image, not only the prompt or earlier version.
Metadata-only requests may use an existing supplied image; do not generate an
unsolicited replacement. If the image is inaccessible, label any prompt-derived
draft provisional, record `NOT_INSPECTED`, and never certify submission readiness.

## Required metadata fields

- **English title:** brief, specific, factual description of the visible primary
  subject and differentiating setting. Prefer fewer than 70 characters. Avoid
  keyword stuffing, technical generation parameters, or unsupported event claims.
- **English keywords:** target 25–40 relevant unique entries in descending
  relevance; put the strongest subject, setting, and buyer-intent terms first,
  especially the first ten. Never exceed the current Adobe maximum (49 at the
  policy check below). Include title concepts only when actually visible or
  clearly supported. State count. Remove duplicates and irrelevant synonyms.
- **Suggested asset type:** `Illustrations` for the signature painterly fantasy
  art. Use `Photos` only for camera-like realistic subjects as described in
  [compliance](stock-compliance.md). Asset type and category are separate fields.
- **Suggested content category:** one current Adobe category, selected for the
  primary subject and intended use, with a short rationale. Validate the exact
  current category label; do not invent a category called "Fantasy".
- **AI labeling:** instruct the Contributor to select `Created using generative
  AI tools`. Do not claim it was selected unless observed in the portal.
- **Fictional declaration:** whether to select `People and Property are
  fictional`, with rationale from actual people/property and their provenance.
- **Model/property releases:** required, not applicable with reason, or pending
  verification. Identify evidence needed; never fabricate releases.
- **Licensing rights:** `VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `FAILED`, evidence references,
  remaining manual steps, and overall readiness under the compliance guide.

If fewer than 25 relevant keywords can be justified, return the honest shorter
draft and flag metadata completion; do not pad with false content. Keep English
metadata even when the conversation is Thai. Instruct the Contributor to use
English as the portal metadata language for these fields.

Do not include artist names, brands, known people, copyrighted characters,
franchises, third-party IP, false editorial/news claims, or misleading locations.
Do not add "Generative AI" or "AI generated" to titles or keywords as a labeling
substitute. Do not describe unseen details, real locations, camera capture, or
commercial rights that have not been established. Recheck metadata after edits.

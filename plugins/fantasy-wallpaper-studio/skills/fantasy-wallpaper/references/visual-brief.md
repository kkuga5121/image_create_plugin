# Current Visual Brief

Use these defaults unless the current user explicitly changes them. They derive
from the latest brief in the 11 November 2026 wallpaper session summary.

- Painterly semi-realistic Fantasy Concept Art across both subject and scene.
- Natural proportions where applicable; controlled digital oil/gouache
  brushwork, nuanced material surfaces, soft light/shadow, atmospheric layers,
  and deliberate lighting.
- Human subjects are adult women, generally one per image. Other valid subjects
  are landscapes without people, animals, spirits, robots, or expressive objects.
  Honor the selected category; do not add a woman to every scene.

  inspiration. Invent a new scene, protagonist, and storytelling moment.

  Leave a small crop margin around important objects, not a reserved clock area.
- Artwork only: no UI, text, logos, watermarks, device mockups, or side-by-side
  montage used in place of separately delivered images.
